# 0031: The vision lives in #105, the plan lives in the brain

**Decision:** Discussion #105 keeps the parts that change rarely: the mission, the goals, what 1.0 means, the non-goals, the principles and the architecture. The brain keeps the parts that change often: the order of the milestones, their versions, their reasons and the open questions. #105's roadmap becomes a short summary that links to [road-to-1-0.md](../milestones/road-to-1-0.md), the epics and the board. Statuses stay on the board.

**Why:** #105 went stale within days. It still named `@ontology-kit/format` and had no versions or 1.0, because it held the plan as well as the vision. The owner agreed to split them when asked whether #105 was stale. One home for each kind of content means two copies can't drift.

**Weighed:** moving the whole vision into the brain, so it's versioned with the code. Not chosen: #105 is pinned and public, and it's where outside readers look first.

**Consequences:** a change of direction edits #105 and adds a comment saying what changed. A change of plan or order goes in the brain only.
