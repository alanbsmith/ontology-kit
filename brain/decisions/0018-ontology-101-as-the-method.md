# 0018: Ontology 101 as the method

**Decision:** okb guides a beginner through the Ontology 101 method, and the meta-KB is built on it.

**Why:** Ontology 101 is a foundational document and gives a good framework for building an ontology. It is meant to be the foundation, not the only source: the aim is to bring in other ontology documents too.
