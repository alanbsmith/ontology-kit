# Subject-matter experts (0.6.0)

## Why

Goal 3 of [0021](../decisions/0021-one-point-zero-is-six-user-goals.md): anyone using a KB, often an expert who doesn't know graphs, can understand it and integrate with it. #105: plain-language pull request summaries, a browsable KB site with evidence on every fact, a contributor skill and relevant competency questions.

## Why now

It follows Serving and Team guardrails in #105's order, and it is the last feature milestone before the soak.

## Done when

The milestone's issues are merged and released as 0.6.0. The owner wants a way to see the KB as a graph, perhaps a playground or sandbox. The plan proposes folding that into spike #19 (a browsable view) and okb site (#108), with a recipe for a local graph database in the loading guide. The owner left it open as a spike ([open question](../open-questions.md)).
