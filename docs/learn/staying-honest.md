# Staying honest

This chapter is about trust. You learn how okb tells you when your ontology breaks a rule, what to do about it, and where each rule comes from. Read it when you've built something and want to know it's sound.

## What you should know

- An [ontology](../GLOSSARY.md#concept-ontology) is the plan of kinds and links. Its [classes](../GLOSSARY.md#concept-class) are kinds of things, and its [slots](../GLOSSARY.md#concept-slot) are the named fields on them.
- A [competency question](../GLOSSARY.md#concept-competency-question) is a question your ontology must be able to answer.
- A [class hierarchy](../GLOSSARY.md#concept-class-hierarchy) stacks kinds with "is a kind of" links.
